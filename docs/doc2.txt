RAG stands for Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

Instead of relying only on parametric knowledge inside a language model,
a RAG system retrieves external documents and uses them as context
for answer generation.

Evaluating RAG quality is not trivial. It usually includes:
- checking whether the retrieved documents are relevant,
- checking whether the final answer is grounded in these documents,
- and measuring answer quality with automatic and human metrics.
