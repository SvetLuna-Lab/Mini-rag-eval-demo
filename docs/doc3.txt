Lexical metrics such as precision, recall, F1-score and Jaccard overlap
can be used as a very simple starting point for RAG evaluation.

They compare the tokens of a predicted answer with the tokens of
a reference answer and compute how much they overlap.

These metrics are not perfect:
- they ignore word order,
- they do not understand synonyms,
- they do not measure factual correctness.

However, they are easy to implement and can be useful for quick,
rough comparisons between different system variants.
